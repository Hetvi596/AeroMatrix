# Experimental method

**Reference data:** the supplied long-format CSV is audited in chunks. Exact duplicate/key hashes cover all 1,820,868 rows; PM2.5/PM10 conflicts are checked by exact grouped values. No duplicates/conflicting targets were found. Negative/nonfinite/incorrect-unit values would be excluded; positive outliers remain in the reference data. Gaps are explicit missing hours, not zeros or interpolated truth. Each station/pollutant's coverage and gaps are in data/audit.json.

**Spatial subset:** five names match official monitoring coordinates. Other station observations remain in the cleaned cache but are not used for spatial training or evaluation. Unknown locations are rejected. PM2.5/PM10 each have a network model and five separately trained station-holdout models. The latter exclude the held-out station from every training target, donor value, model selection and calibration record. No station ID or target pollutant history is a feature. A held-out station's coordinates and independent reanalysis at that coordinate are permitted.

**Forecast:** predict the next hourly reading from observations at or before cutoff. A two-hour maximum donor age permits strictly forward carry with age recorded; IDW uses precisely those donors. Distinct donors closer than 50 m to target are excluded. Require at least two donors, nearest within 20 km and all within 30 km. Persistence uses the target's last visible reading no older than six hours. It is never available for an entirely withheld location. API records additionally enforce available_at <= cutoff.

**Features:** target latitude/longitude, cyclical local hour/month, weekday, IDW, donor mean/std/range/count, nearest/mean distance and maximum donor age. A weather candidate adds seven ERA5 variables delayed 120 days and aligned backward to UTC whole hour (30 minutes before the corresponding IST cutoff). Weather is never from a later valid time. Training-only median imputation plus missing indicators applies to missing weather. No pollution target is imputed.

**Weather caution:** ERA5 finalization typically follows ERA5T by 2–3 months. Using final archives contemporaneously would leak unavailable information. The 120-day delay is deliberately conservative and only provides past weather context, not current conditions. Exact original as-of vintages were not obtainable, so revision/latency caveats remain. A no-weather candidate is always evaluated and may win.

**Splits:** training target times strictly before 2024-10-01; selection cutoffs from 2024-10-01 with target times before 2024-12-01; calibration cutoffs from 2024-12-01 with targets before 2025-02-01; testing cutoffs from 2025-02-01. Boundary targets are purged. Train on earlier times; never randomly split reference observations. Some late-opening stations have no training labels naturally. Every holdout is independently trained/tuned with fixed algorithms.

**Selection:** fixed ExtraTrees and histogram-gradient-boosting candidates, each with and without delayed weather. Select lowest validation MAE. Training includes original donor views plus a 30%-masked view using seed 17. Counts distinguish unique target observations from augmented feature rows. Calibration uses a later, unused chronological segment and the finite-sample 90% absolute residual quantile. Predictions are clipped nonnegative. Intervals are marginal; temporal dependence, spatial shift and outages invalidate any unconditional coverage guarantee. Test coverage/width are reported rather than assumed.

**Outages:** 0, 10, 30, 50, 70% nominal probability; seeds 17/29/43. Random observations, station-specific 12-hour blocks and whole-station masks. Network runs all patterns; unseen-location runs random masks at every level/seed. A target is always excluded from spatial donors even at 0% loss. Target labels stay independently unchanged. Whole-station losses round to station counts. Realized valid-input losses are in evaluation/loss_realization.json. No active physical outage is asserted.

**Comparisons:** available-case metrics show each method's coverage. `common_three` uses the identical intersection for ML/IDW/persistence; `common_spatial` uses identical ML/IDW cases; `target_missing_common` isolates cases whose current target input was unavailable. An unseen station has no persistence, so common-three n=0 is explicitly unsupported. Metrics include MAE/RMSE/bias/R² (when meaningful), counts, availability, station coverage and periods. Repeat seeds measure mask sensitivity, not independent population replicates.

**Interventions:** the model forecasts concentrations, not causal effects. The sensitivity function requires named cost/effect sources, enumerates feasible subsets and varies interval lower/central/upper, effect multiplier, budget and action availability. Bundled demonstration inputs are explicitly hypothetical mathematical assumptions, not environmental measurements or validated policy effects. Real planner stability remains conditional on externally supplied justified effects and costs.
